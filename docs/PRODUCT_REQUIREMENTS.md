# Product requirements

Single-branch gym for owner, receptionist and members. Indonesian interface. Light staff workspace, dark member portal. Palette: charcoal, neutral off-white, selective champagne. Self-hosted Instrument Sans through the existing Vite font integration.

Implemented: session authentication; server role checks; owner team management; members with optional login; search by name/number/phone; status filter and pagination; owner plans; price snapshots; full manual settlement; no-loss renewal; identity-verified staff attendance; duplicate/visit-limit rules; shifts and discrepancies; own member data; receipt; real collections chart/KPIs; CSV; audit.

Pending: QR/gateway/webhooks/notification delivery, PWA, photo upload, password reset and link activation, partial payments/refunds/taxes/discounts, membership freezes/extensions, full configurable gym settings UI, extended reporting charts and plan/date/sort filters on members. No external messaging is sent.
